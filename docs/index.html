The study

Understanding mental health through everyday smartphone use

DETECTMiND explores whether patterns in how we use our smartphones can help predict when someone may be at risk of anxiety or depression, so that support can reach people sooner.

Study title
“A Multi-Task, Multi-Objective Decision Analytics Framework for Predicting At-Risk Mental States of Anxiety and Depression”

FAQ

Common questions

Who is running this study?

DETECTMiND is a joint study by the University of Southampton and the University of Portsmouth, led by Anoop Kadan and Linnea Stenlund-Roderick. It has ethics approval under ERGO number 117353.

How do I take part?

Read the Participant Information Sheet, complete the Consent Form, and email it to detectmind@soton.ac.uk, a.kadan@soton.ac.uk or lsr1n24@soton.ac.uk. The form above prepares the email for you.

Which email address should I use?

Please use your institutional email ID, for example abcd@soton.ac.uk.

When will I get the app?

We will reach out to you and provide the app link after you submit the consent form, prior to the study starting.

What phone do I need?

The DETECTMiND app runs on Android phones with Android 8.0 or later.

What does the app record?

The Participant Information Sheet explains what the study collects and why, and the app’s privacy policy lists exactly what the app records and what it never records.
